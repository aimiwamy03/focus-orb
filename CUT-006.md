# CUT-006: Calm states cleanup (UX audit fixes 1 to 10)

Date: 2026-10-21. Base: PR #8 branch (`cursor/cut-008-square-orb-803a`).

In: all 10 audit items. Out: Google verification, privacy page, new features.

Amy prefers no em dashes in copy and minimal, calm, intentional screens for each state.

## Acceptance

- **AC1 (item 1)** Ask state has one primary way to start: the center prompt (task + duration + Start). Clicking the orb also starts, but no copy competes. Corner chrome stays hidden until the mouse moves.
- **AC2 (item 8)** Copy is consistent. The status line and button use the same verb. No en or em dashes (times like 2:00-3:00 with a hyphen, or "to"). "Full" becomes "Fullscreen". Audit all user-facing strings.
- **AC3 (item 3)** Shortcut hints appear only in the idle Ask state. Never in focusing, meeting soon, in meeting, completed, or break.
- **AC4 (item 10)** One teaching place for shortcuts: the "?" Shortcuts panel. Keep the contextual inline keycaps next to their control (Enter to start, Space to pause). Remove the duplicate list from Settings. The one-time card shows once in idle Ask only and does not come back after dismissal or first use.
- **AC5 (item 4)** Ending early shows a calm completed state ("Focused 18 of 50 min on <task>") with Done and Start another, matching the full-completion screen.
- **AC6 (item 5)** All text meets WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1, or 3:1 for >=18px) on the background. Add a test that checks the palette tokens.
- **AC7 (item 2)** "Connect your calendar" calls Google sign-in directly when `NEXT_PUBLIC_GOOGLE_CONFIGURED` is true (it is on prod). It only falls back to plain copy plus a sample day when not configured.
- **AC8 (item 6)** Settings is trimmed to real settings: calendars, default duration, mini orb, sign out (show signed-in email). No Start button, no shortcut list, no jargon ("scatter window" becomes "Warn me before meetings").
- **AC9 (item 7)** `?mock=...` anchors to a fixed demo time (11:30) unless `?now=` is given, and shows a small "Sample day" label.
- **AC10 (item 9)** Mini only cannot strand the user. It is only available while the PiP is actually open. If the PiP closes, the main screen restores automatically. Any fallback shows "Open floating orb" and Esc restores.
